#K-OS
#Thinking #Visualized

An external cognitive operating system that captures, connects, visualizes, and evolves knowledge to improve engineering reasoning, decision making, and lifelong learning.

**Mission**

	- To think better.
	
	- To visualize knowledge.
	
	- To continuously improve.

**Principles**

	- Capture with intention.
	- Connect everything meaningful.
	- Think from first principles.
	- Evidence over assumption.
	- Every note must earn its place.
	- Knowledge should evolve.
	- Failures are knowledge.
	- Reuse before rewriting.
