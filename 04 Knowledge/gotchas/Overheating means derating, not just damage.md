# Overheating means derating, not just damage
**What I did:** answered "motor too hot → magnets demagnetise".
**Why incomplete:** demag is the risk being avoided; the actual system behaviour is derating (MC cuts torque).
**Fix:** for any "what happens if X gets hot" question, answer the behaviour first, then the damage it prevents.
