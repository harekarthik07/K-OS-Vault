## Key takeaways
1. Power per cell sets cell heat; pack voltage sets conductor losses.
2. Temperature, not current, ages a cell. Tmax and ΔT are what CAE must predict.
3. When things get hot the bike **derates**; that's the rider-visible behaviour.
